## What could I be wrong about?
- **The expected return is lower than the headline.** The 20–28% CAGR figure is the backtest at **0.15% cost per side**. The paper account charges **0.9% per side** (`paper_cost_per_side`), and the same out-of-sample test at high fees gives **~18.4% CAGR** (`results/summary.csv`). Judge the rule against roughly 18%/yr and a −25% to −30% worst drawdown, not 28%.
- **The backtest is on BTC/USD; the bot trades BTC/AUD.** AUD moves shift where price sits relative to its averages, so signals and trade dates will differ from the backtest. The AUD version hasn't been tested out of sample.
- **Cash can be the wrong call.** Trend filters buy back late. With RSI at 18.6 after a −26% month, a sharp rebound would leave the rule waiting for +17% before it buys. That's the cost it accepted in 2022–26, and it's real.
- **The GitHub delay might be temporary.** It could be specific to a new repo or this week's load. If so, PR #1 is cheap insurance that may not have been needed.
- **There may be a decision task I can't see.** A task stored locally in the desktop app wouldn't show up in my list. But no decision files or PRs exist, so either way nothing has been produced.
- **Paying for itself.** On $20, even a good year at ~18% is about **$3.60**. That covers no subscription, API or other cost, and these numbers give no reason to think otherwise. This is a cheap, honest experiment in whether Claude's research beats a dumb rule. It is not an income source.
